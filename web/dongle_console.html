Actions

Pairing mode is the same as holding the dongle's button. Rebooting drops the USB link; the console reconnects when the dongle comes back.

Paired controllers

  • Connect the dongle to list its paired controllers.

Settings

Xbox-style physical layout on the Switch.
0 % Radial deadzone, 0–50 % of full travel.
100 % Overall brightness: scanning, pairing and connected.
25 % Steady level once a controller is connected, as a share of the brightness above. 0 turns it off while playing.
Debugging aid: toggles the LED with each report forwarded to the host instead of holding it steady.
1–31 printable ASCII characters. Applies after reboot.
